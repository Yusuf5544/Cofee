Our Story

Founded in 2010, Brew & Bean started as a small neighborhood coffee shop with a simple mission: to serve exceptional coffee in a warm, welcoming environment.

Over the years, we've built relationships with coffee farmers across the globe, ensuring we source only the highest quality beans while supporting sustainable farming practices.

Our baristas are passionate about their craft, carefully preparing each cup to highlight the unique flavors of our carefully selected beans.

Whether you're stopping by for your morning espresso or settling in for an afternoon of work, we're dedicated to making your experience memorable.
